--- title: The Cosmos theme date: 2026-11-07 categories: - website - features tags: - blogtini - themes - svg - space featured: themes/cosmos.jpg featuredcaption: 'The Cosmos theme: a vector arcade game, in space' theme: cosmos comment: --- **INSERT COIN.** Blogtini has a third theme of its very own -- after [Librarian](../2026-10-librarian-theme/) & [Dossier](../2026-11-dossier-theme/), made from scratch, for blogtini. **Cosmos** turns your blog into an early-80s vector arcade game, in space: glowing green lines on black, like Battlezone, Star Wars & Moon Patrol. You're already seeing this post in Cosmos (unless you've picked another theme). **[Try it on the whole site](#theme=cosmos)** -- your pick lasts 24 hours, just for you. Or flip thru every theme, live, in the [previewer](../preview/#cosmos). To make it your own site's theme: `theme: cosmos` in your `config.yml`. ## Look up Behind every page is a star field -- three layers of stars, each drifting at its own speed as you scroll. It's subtle: lean in. Some stars twinkle. And somewhere up there, if you look closely, is a pale blue dot. (It's not the only thing hiding in the sky.) On the home page, you're driving: wireframe mountains, a volcano, a crescent moon, & a grid floor rolling toward you, w/ wireframe pyramids sliding past. And at the bottom of every page, our own lunar rover rolls along over the craters -- a pressurized cabin, a satellite dish, six wheels, a headlight beam ... & one more thing up top. For blog-*tini*. 🍸 ## Your posts, as a star map Every post gets its **own planet** -- its size, tilt, color, rings or craters all come from the post itself, so a post always has the same one. Then: - **SECTOR 2026-11-07** -- its sector number. (Look again. Yep: it's the date.) - its tags & categories, as **star charts** - & a **FUEL** gauge: the reading time Prior & next posts? **< PREV SECTOR** & **NEXT SECTOR >**. ## Mission control At the end of every page: - **Scan**: search, w/ a radar scope. Every post is a blip on it -- type a search, & the matching posts light up. - **Star charts** (tags), **Galaxies** (categories), & **Warp** -- the theme picker. - And of course, **HIGH SCORES**: the longest posts, ten points a word. ## Inspiration *Cosmos*, by Carl Sagan, is Tracey's favorite book ever. Look up at the stars! And then there were all those quarters, fed into vector arcade games. ## Fine print - Pick your phosphor: **green**, **amber** or **cyan** -- buttons at the top. Your pick sticks. - No images, no fonts to download: the stars, terrain, planets, rover & the blocky letters are all drawn in svg, right in your browser. Like everything in blogtini, nothing calls out to anyone else's servers. - Prefer less motion? If your device asks for reduced motion, everything holds still. *"We are made of star-stuff"* -- Carl Sagan **GAME OVER.** Play again?
