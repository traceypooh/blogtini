(Hover it.)" theme: dossier comment: --- **MEMORANDUM** -- for all personnel. Clearance: public. Blogtini has a second theme of its very own. Like [Librarian](../2026-10-librarian-theme/), it was made from scratch, for blogtini, by ~~Agent T~~ -- and you won't find it anywhere else. **Dossier** turns your blog into a drawer of classified case files, straight out of the spy movies: typed folders, red rubber stamps, words blacked out, & every photo a frame grab from a surveillance camera. This file is already showing itself in Dossier (unless you've picked another theme). **[Try it on the whole site](#theme=dossier)** -- your pick lasts 24 hours, just for you. Or flip thru every theme, live, in the [previewer](../preview/#dossier). To make it your own site's theme: `theme: dossier` in your `config.yml`. ![Case files: each post a file folder, w/ its stamps](../img/themes/dossier.jpg) ## The case files Every post is a file folder: - a **tab**, w/ a file number made from the post's date -- eg: `FILE NO. 26-1106-D` - typed **fields**: subject, filed, & agent. No author? The agent's name is ~~classified~~. - a paper-clipped photo, in black & white - a summary -- w/ a few small words blacked out. (They're still there, for screen readers & search. Hover a black bar to peek.) - its **known associates**: its tags & categories - & two stamps. A classification marking -- TOP SECRET, EYES ONLY, BURN AFTER READING, & more -- each post always gets the same one. And **BRIEFING: 4 MIN**: the post's reading time. Hover a photo w/ your mouse: a spy camera's **zoom lens** -- 3x, crosshairs, & back in color. ## The file Open a post, & you get the whole file: its stamp, fields, a routing slip at the end ("DISTRIBUTION: File · Director · ~~Agent K~~ · You"), & -- if you look closely -- a coffee ring someone left on it. The post's main photo is **Exhibit A**, as seen thru a camera's viewfinder: focus brackets, a split-image focusing screen in the middle, the camera's timestamp, & a little ● REC light -- see the top of this file. Hover it, & the viewfinder fades away: the photo, up close, in color. Other photos in a post are Exhibit B, C, ... -- captioned w/ their alt text, like the case files above. Want to black out words yourself? In markdown, `~~like this~~` -- Dossier draws it as a redaction. ## Inspiration Tracey's dad has had a life-long love of spy books, novels and movies. He made sure his four kids all read the best spy books and watched the best spy movies -- some repeatedly! ## Fine print - It's nearly all black & white -- the one color is the red of the stamp pad. At night (or w/ the Scheme lightbulb), it switches to **night ops**. - Typed in Special Elite & Courier Prime, stamped in Allerta Stencil -- all self-hosted, like everything in blogtini. Nothing calls out to anyone else's servers. - The movie markings, not any real agency: no names, no seals. This document is unclassified. Probably. -- End of memo --
